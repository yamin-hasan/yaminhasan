Understanding your market

Make it easier for the right customers to understand your service.

Whether customers call it pressure washing, jet washing or pressure cleaning, they want to know what surfaces you handle, where you work and how to arrange a visit. Your online presence should answer those questions clearly.

Customers are not looking for marketing terminology. They are looking for a capable business, a clear description of the work and enough useful information to decide whether to get in touch.

Common challenges

Different names for the same service

UK customers may search for jet washing while Australian customers often use pressure cleaning. Your service language should reflect the market you serve.

A wide range of surfaces

Driveways, patios, block paving and exterior surfaces each have different expectations. A broad, vague description can leave customers unsure.

Service areas need clarity

Customers need to understand whether you cover their neighbourhood, town or wider region before they make contact.
