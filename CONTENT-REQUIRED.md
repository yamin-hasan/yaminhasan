# Content required before launch

The site deliberately avoids invented credentials, client outcomes, contact details and testimonials. Supply or confirm the following before treating the profile and contact flow as final.

## Professional profile
- A short first-person biography and preferred professional introduction.
- Verified employment history, if it should appear.
- Verified client engagements or case studies, with permission to publish.
- Verifiable qualifications, certifications or training, if relevant.
- Measurable achievements with evidence and permission to disclose.
- Genuine professional profile links for `sameAs` structured data, if any.

## Contact and privacy
- The email address that should receive enquiries (set as `CONTACT_TO_EMAIL` in Cloudflare).
- A verified sender address/domain for Resend (`CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL`).
- Full legal data-controller identity and privacy contact details.
- Confirmed retention period and final third-party data-processing list.
- Legal review of the privacy notice for the UK and Australian markets.

## Brand assets
- Approved headshot or portrait, if desired.
- Approved social sharing image (the current project uses original vector artwork).
- Any genuine testimonials, with customer permission and attribution details.

## Business details
- Confirm preferred spelling/capitalisation of the professional title.
- Confirm the exact scope of services and any exclusions.
- Confirm whether the complimentary visibility review is offered without conditions.
