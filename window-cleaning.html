Understanding your market

Make it easier for the right customers to understand your service.

For window cleaning businesses, a clear online presence can support both one-off enquiries and the longer-term relationships behind regular cleaning rounds. Customers want to understand the service, the coverage and how to get started.

Customers are not looking for marketing terminology. They are looking for a capable business, a clear description of the work and enough useful information to decide whether to get in touch.

Common challenges

Customers should be able to understand whether you offer regular visits, one-off cleans or tailored arrangements.

Residential and commercial work varies

Trust supports repeat decisions
