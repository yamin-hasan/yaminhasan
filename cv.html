Professional profile

Helping specialist service businesses communicate their value clearly.

My professional focus is the online visibility and business presentation of exterior cleaning companies. I work with owners who want customers to understand what they offer, where they work and why their business is worth considering.

The work is deliberately specialised. Pressure washing and pressure cleaning, gutter cleaning and window cleaning each have their own customer expectations, service language and commercial considerations. Understanding those differences helps keep recommendations relevant.

Core areas of focus

  • Google Maps presence and Google business listing management
  • Business information accuracy and service descriptions
  • Website service-page clarity and customer journeys
  • Business reputation and genuine review strategy
  • Service-area presentation for UK and Australian markets
  • Ongoing visibility review and practical prioritisation

Working methodology

I begin by understanding the business: its services, customers, coverage and current presentation. From there, I identify the gaps that matter most, agree a realistic order of work and communicate changes in straightforward terms. Recommendations are grounded in the information available and in the services the business genuinely provides.

Professional approach

Clear communication, careful handling of business information and realistic expectations are central to the work. I do not promise specific Google positions, guaranteed enquiries or outcomes that depend on factors outside my control.

This profile intentionally includes only confirmed positioning and areas of focus. Employment history, client engagements, qualifications, certifications and measurable achievements have not been supplied and are not claimed here.
