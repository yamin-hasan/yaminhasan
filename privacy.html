Privacy notice.

How information submitted through this website is intended to be handled.

Review-ready template: This page is a general privacy notice draft and is not legal advice. It must be reviewed and completed by the site owner before publication, particularly to confirm the controller identity, contact details, providers, retention periods and applicable legal obligations.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

This website is operated under the name Yamin Hasan. The data controller's full legal identity and contact details have not been supplied and must be added before this notice is treated as final. You can use the contact page to submit an enquiry, but that form should not be used for urgent or sensitive information.

2. Information collected

If you use the contact form, the information you choose to provide may include your name, email address, business name, business website, country, business type, business location, Google Business Profile URL and message. Basic technical information may also be processed by the website hosting provider when a page or form endpoint is requested.

3. Why information is used

Enquiry information is intended to be used to review and respond to your request, communicate about the services you asked about and maintain a record of the enquiry where reasonably necessary. Information should not be used for unrelated marketing without an appropriate lawful basis and any consent required by applicable law.

4. How information is processed

The website is designed to send contact form submissions to a configured email delivery provider. The provider, account configuration and any additional storage or processing arrangements must be confirmed by the site owner before launch. Cloudflare may process technical request data as part of hosting and security services.

5. Storage and retention

Enquiry details should be retained only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, maintain appropriate business records and meet applicable legal requirements. The site owner must set and document a specific retention period and ensure copies in email accounts or other systems are handled consistently.

6. Third-party services

This website may use Cloudflare Pages for hosting, Resend for email delivery after configuration, Google Fonts for typography, and CDN-hosted scripts for icons and animation. Those providers may process technical data in accordance with their own terms and privacy documentation. Confirm the final provider list and settings before publication.

7. International transfers

Because the website is intended to serve enquiries from the United Kingdom and Australia, information may be processed in countries where hosting or service providers operate. The site owner must confirm the applicable safeguards and disclose relevant transfer information where required.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or make a complaint to a relevant privacy regulator. The precise rights and process depend on the circumstances and jurisdiction.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect enquiry information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Contact and updates

The site owner's verified privacy contact details must be inserted here before publication. This notice should be reviewed whenever the website's forms, providers or data practices change.
